I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at The University of Texas at Austin with a Ph.D. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of California, Riverside. My work focuses on thermal transport and lattice dynamics in functional oxides and solid-state ionic conductors.

I combine crystal and thin-film growth, materials characterization, and data-driven modeling in Python and MATLAB. My research connects experiments at national laboratories with cross-disciplinary materials and device teams.
