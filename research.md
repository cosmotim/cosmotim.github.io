## Thermal transport in solid electrolytes

My work investigates heat conduction in Li-ion and Na-ion solid electrolytes, including garnet, perovskite, and NaSICON structures. I identified diffuson-mediated thermal transport in Li-ion conductors and developed a two-channel phonon–diffuson model to explain anomalous temperature dependence.

[Read the Applied Physics Reviews review](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0318328)

## Single-crystal growth

I built a floating-zone crystal growth workflow from scratch, producing centimeter-scale LLZTO single crystals. These samples enabled phonon-resolved neutron scattering measurements that had been infeasible with polycrystalline material.

[Crystal growth and electrochemical studies](https://doi.org/10.1007/s42864-022-00176-z)

## Neutron scattering & lattice dynamics

I authored two successful beam time proposals and led inelastic neutron-scattering experiments using TAX at HFIR and ARCS at SNS, Oak Ridge National Laboratory. The work resolved the full phonon dispersion of a garnet solid electrolyte and provided benchmarks for first-principles calculations.

I developed automated analysis pipelines in Mantid and Python and coordinated experiments across approximately seven research groups, including collaborators at UCR, UT Austin, ORNL, ETH Zürich, and TU Wien.

[Read the PRX Energy paper](https://doi.org/10.1103/6wj2-kzhh)

## Electrochemistry & computational materials

Earlier projects included composite battery separators, CR2032 cell testing, electrochemical impedance spectroscopy, and first-principles modeling of defects in olivine cathodes. I have also designed strain-control apparatus and used Monte Carlo simulations to study atomic motion.
